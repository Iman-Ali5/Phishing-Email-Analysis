# Security Recommendations

## Introduction

Based on the phishing email investigation, the following recommendations can help individuals and organisations reduce the risk of phishing attacks.

## Recommendations

### 1. Check the Sender's Email Address

Always check the full sender address before trusting an email. Be cautious of unfamiliar or suspicious email domains.

### 2. Avoid Clicking Suspicious Links

Do not click links in unexpected emails. If an email claims to be from your bank, visit the bank's official website directly instead.

### 3. Be Careful with Urgent Messages

Be suspicious of emails that pressure you into acting quickly or threaten account suspension.

### 4. Never Share Sensitive Information

Never provide passwords, banking details or security codes through links in unexpected emails.

### 5. Report Suspicious Emails

Report suspected phishing emails to your organisation's IT or security team so they can investigate and protect other users.

## Conclusion

Following these recommendations can help reduce the risk of phishing attacks, protect sensitive information and improve overall security awareness.
