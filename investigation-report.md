# Phishing Email Investigation

## 1. Introduction

The purpose of this investigation is to analyse a simulated phishing email and identify signs that could indicate a phishing attempt.

## 2. Email Analysis

### Finding 1: Urgent Language

The subject line uses urgent language to grab the recipient's attention and pressure them into taking immediate action.

The email also threatens to suspend the recipient's bank account within 24 hours. This creates fear and encourages the recipient to act without thinking carefully.

This is a social engineering technique commonly used in phishing attacks.

### Finding 2: Suspicious Sender Address

The sender address could be used to impersonate a bank and make the email appear genuine. Someone receiving the email might trust it because it appears to come from a bank security team.

Checking the full sender address and comparing its domain with the bank's official website can help identify possible impersonation.

### Finding 3: Suspicious Link

The email contains a link asking the recipient to verify their identity. This is suspicious because scammers may use fake websites to steal personal information, login details or banking information.

The link also uses HTTP rather than HTTPS, which means the connection is not encrypted. This is another warning sign, although HTTPS alone does not guarantee that a website is legitimate.

### Finding 4: Generic Greeting

The email starts with "Dear Customer" instead of addressing the recipient by name. This could mean that the same email has been sent to multiple people rather than being personalised. This is a potential warning sign of a phishing email.

### Finding 5: Request for Personal Information

The email asks the recipient to confirm their identity through a link. This is suspicious because scammers may use fake websites to collect personal information, login details and banking details. They could then use this information to gain unauthorised access to accounts or commit fraud.

## 3. Conclusion

Based on the findings, the email shows several signs of a phishing attempt. These include urgent language, a suspicious sender address, a suspicious link, a generic greeting and a request for personal information.

The email is designed to pressure the recipient into clicking a link and sharing sensitive information.

The recipient should avoid clicking the link, avoid providing any personal details and report the email as suspicious.
